Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Cortes ("the app") runs entirely on the user's own computer. The developer does not operate a server for the app and does not receive, collect, store or sell any user data.

What the app stores, and where

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens, after you connect your account. They are saved only in a file on your computer and are used only to call TikTok on your behalf.
  • Your TikTok display name, shown inside the app so you know which account is connected.
  • Your settings, and the video clips you create, saved in folders on your computer.

What is sent to TikTok

Only when you review a clip and confirm the upload, the app sends that video file and the caption you approved to TikTok through the Content Posting API. Nothing is uploaded without your explicit confirmation.

Optional AI features

If you enable an AI provider (such as Google Gemini, a local Ollama model, or Anthropic Claude), the app may send a few still frames from a clip, and short text such as the stream title, to the provider you selected, using your own key or your own computer. Each provider's own privacy policy applies. If you do not enable AI, nothing is sent to AI providers.

Revoking access

You can disconnect TikTok at any time inside the app (Settings, Disconnect), or by removing the app in your TikTok account's security settings. Deleting the app's folder deletes all locally stored data.
